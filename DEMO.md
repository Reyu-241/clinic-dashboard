Hook: Clinic Operations Dashboard is a patient-and-appointment dashboard for small clinic owners.
Problem: Without it, a clinic owner must piece together patient records, visit statuses, and growth signals from separate lists.
Walk: Sign in > create a patient > edit it > review the charts > sign out.
Decision: I chose a six-month new-patient bar chart and this-month appointment-status donut because they surface growth and visit mix; since the initial dashboard concept, I added treatment tracking, a tomorrow view, and public booking requests.
Limits: Appointments can overlap and reminders are not automated; next, prevent conflicts, add reminders, and add staff roles.
