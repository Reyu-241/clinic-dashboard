Hook: Clinic Operations Dashboard is a patient-and-appointment dashboard for small clinic owners.

Problem: Without it, a clinic owner must piece together patient records, visit statuses, and growth signals from separate lists.

Walk: Sign in > create a patient > edit it > review the charts > sign out.

Decision: built with react and nextjs. I chose to keep things simple to catch errors quickly, I chose the pie chart to represnt a simiple incication of statuses and the bar chart for patents per month.

Limits: Appointments can overlap and reminders are not automated; next, prevent conflicts, add reminders, and add staff roles and add more options for clients and an option for admin/ docters using the app to create an account for the admin side.
